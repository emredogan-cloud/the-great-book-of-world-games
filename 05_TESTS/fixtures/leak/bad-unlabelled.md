# Oware

Place the board between you so that six hollows face each player. Each
player takes four seeds and drops one into every hollow on their side.
On your turn you lift every seed from one hollow and sow them one by one,
anticlockwise around the board. If the last seed lands in a hollow on
your opponent's side that then holds two or three seeds, you capture
them. If you cannot move, play passes to the other side. The player who
holds more than half the seeds wins the game. If neither player can
capture for a full circuit, the game is a draw and each side takes what
remains in front of them.
