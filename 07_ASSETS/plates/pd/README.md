# Public-domain and CC0 plates (GBK-02 recovery, 26 September 2026)

These replace the eleven plates the 2026 audit flagged (WG-003 duplicate Jan-ken plate; WG-033 wrong
equipment; WG-034 photoreal AI images of members of living peoples). No image here was generated or
altered by AI. Scans were cropped to the figure, levelled (paper to white, ink to black) and padded
with white to the 3:2 plate shape; nothing inside a figure was redrawn. Figure captions printed in the
source were left out; the book prints its own caption and credit.

The Culin pages are cited by the entries themselves (Set-dilth pp. 88–89 and the figures on p. 87; Zohn Ahl
pp. 124–126; Totolospi pp. 160–162; Picaria pp. 797–798; Awithlaknannai pp. 800–801). Totolospi's plate is
fig. 194, the oval board the entry describes, not Culin's fig. 1095, which shows the unrelated checkers-like
game the Tewa also called totolospi. The Forbes frontispiece was checked square by square against the
Chaturaji set-up diagram (Green a1–d1, Red h1–h4, Black e8–h8, Yellow a5–a8, boats in the corners) and agrees.
