# Drawn plates (GBK-02 recovery, 26 September 2026)

Achi and Mū Tōrere have no historical picture that is free to reproduce: the only printed account of
Achi (H. J. R. Murray, 1952) is still in copyright, and the Commons drawings of Mū Tōrere are modern
CC BY-SA work. Their plates are therefore the book's own board drawings, rendered by 04_BUILD/boards.py
from the entries' diagram specs (achi-worked without its arrow, labels or the taken piece; mutorere-setup
as printed), at a 100 mm board span, rasterised at ×6 and padded to 3:2. They are labelled on the page
as drawn for this book. No AI was used.
