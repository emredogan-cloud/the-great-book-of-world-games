# Jayne 1906 — Cat's-cradle figures (public domain)

Source: Caroline Furness Jayne, *String Figures: A Study of Cat's-Cradle in Many Lands*
(New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1906), "Real Cat's-Cradle", pp. 324–336, figs. 741–758.
Scans: Internet Archive item `stringfiguresstu00jayn` (leaves 390–402).

Drawings: by Mrs Morris Cotgrave Betts, credited in Jayne's preface (p. vi: "for her skill and
accuracy in the drawings"); her own name and dates are not established (her husband, the
Philadelphia architect Morris Cotgrave Betts, lived 1875–1936).

Rights: published in the United States in 1906, so public domain in the United States. For this
US-origin work by a non-EEA author, the EU and the UK apply the comparison of terms (Term
Directive 2006/116/EC art. 7(1); CDPA 1988 s. 12(6)) and treat it as public domain too. A small
residual risk remains only in countries that do not compare terms, if the illustrator died after
1955. Credit line used in the book: "drawings by Mrs Morris Cotgrave Betts for Caroline Furness
Jayne, *String Figures* (1906)".
